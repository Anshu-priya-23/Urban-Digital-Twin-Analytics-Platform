**Project Title**: Smart City Urban Traffic & Air Quality Analytics Platform
The Problem: Modern cities struggle with interconnected issues. Heavy traffic causes delays, which in turn spikes dangerous air pollution (PM2.5 and NO2). City planners need data-driven insights to know where and when to intervene.

**The Solution**: I built a data pipeline that integrates traffic counts, weather indices, and pollution metrics.

**The Tech Stack**:

**Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn): Used to clean data, run exploratory statistical correlations, and engineered features.

**Machine Learning** (Scikit-Learn): Built a Random Forest Regressor model achieving high accuracy in predicting vehicle speeds based on time and rainfall metrics.

**Power BI**: Developed an interactive, executive-facing Business Intelligence dashboard for real-time scenario filtering.

**Key Recommendations for the City**:

Deploy traffic mitigation forces on Weekdays specifically between 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM due to systemic rush-hour spikes.

Prioritize green infrastructure (like planting trees or installing air filters) around Road 001 as it statistically holds the worst air quality profiles.
